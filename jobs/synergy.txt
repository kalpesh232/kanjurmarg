-> difference between merge, join and concatenate
### Corrected Explanation:

- **Merge**: Combines DataFrames based on common columns or indices. By default, it returns only matching rows (inner join).  
- **Join**: Combines DataFrames based on index. Can specify left, right, or inner join.  
- **Concatenate**: Stacks DataFrames vertically or horizontally. Fills missing values with NaN.
